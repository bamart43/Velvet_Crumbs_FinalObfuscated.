Our Story

At Velvet Crumb, we don't just make desserts—we craft experiences. Every cookie, cake, and crumb is made from scratch with premium ingredients, a whole lot of love, and a dash of creativity.

Whether you're craving classic comfort or something a little unexpected, our menu is full of sweet surprises designed to satisfy. From gooey brownies and butter-soft cookies to elegant tarts and seasonal specials, there's something here for every kind of sweet tooth.

We believe desserts should be as beautiful as they are delicious—and that every bite should feel like a moment worth savoring.

What We Offer

  • Small-batch, handcrafted desserts
  • Locally sourced ingredients wherever possible
  • Custom orders for events, gifts, or just because
  • Specialized delivery options available

Customer Favorites

Signature Velvet Cupcakes

Moist, fluffy, and topped with our signature whipped cream cheese frosting—these cupcakes are a fan favorite for birthdays, events, or anytime cravings. Available in classic Red Velvet, Chocolate Ganache, and rotating seasonal flavors.

Brown Butter Chocolate Chunk Cookies

Crispy on the edges, gooey in the center, and loaded with chunks of dark chocolate—these cookies strike the perfect balance between nostalgic and gourmet. Baked fresh daily in small batches.

Vanilla Bean Cheesecake Bites

Creamy, dreamy, and perfectly portioned. These bite-sized cheesecakes feature a buttery graham crust and a rich vanilla bean filling, finished with your choice of fruit compote or caramel drizzle.
